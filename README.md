# 💧 BharatJal AI
### AI-Powered Village Water Risk Intelligence System

BharatJal AI is an AI-driven groundwater risk prediction platform designed to help rural communities and policymakers proactively manage water stress at the village level.

The system predicts groundwater depletion 3–6 months in advance using time-series forecasting and multi-variable environmental modeling.

---

## 🚩 Problem Statement

Most existing groundwater monitoring systems display only historical data.  
There is no predictive intelligence at the village level to prevent water crises before they occur.

---

## 💡 Solution Overview

BharatJal AI transforms static dashboards into a predictive decision-support system.

- 3–6 month groundwater forecasting  
- Village-level risk scoring  
- AI-based mitigation insights  
- Early alert system  

---

## 🧠 Unique Value Proposition (USP)

- Predictive + Explainable AI  
- Village-level intelligence  
- Preventive water governance  

**Framework:**  
Predict → Explain → Act
